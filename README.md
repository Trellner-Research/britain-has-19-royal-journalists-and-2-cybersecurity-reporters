# Britain has 19 royal journalists on file and 2 cybersecurity reporters

JournalistLabs read the job titles of the 12,094 UK journalists in its base (September 2026). 19 titles name the royals. 2 name cybersecurity.

- The 19 royal titles: 8 correspondents, 8 editors, 3 reporters.
- GB News has the most royal titles (4 of its 85 UK rows), then the Daily Express (3 of 155).
- Of the 20 largest UK outlets in the file, none has a cybersecurity title and 9 have a royal one.
- Worldwide, 23 of 127,602 journalists carry a cybersecurity title, spread across 17 outlets. Politico's 4 is the largest such desk among the 50 biggest newsrooms.
- Widening the search from "cybersecurity" to thirteen security terms moves the world count from 23 to 30 and leaves the UK at 2.

Both UK counts are floors. Wikipedia lists 5 BBC royal correspondents and 1 BBC cyber correspondent, and none of the 6 is in the base.
